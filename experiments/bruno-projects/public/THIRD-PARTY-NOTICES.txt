Outer-world neutral lettering: scene-sans.ttf is a static weight-600
instance of the supplied Noto Sans SC subset; scene-latin.ttf is a
static weight-800 instance of the supplied Nunito subset. Generated
with fontTools; glyph outlines are not redrawn. These do not override
the original fonts inside the resume or portfolio projects.

Active Chinese handwriting: Xiaolai Regular v3.126, lxgw/kose-font.
https://github.com/lxgw/kose-font/releases/tag/v3.126
Subset of the author's original font, without redrawing or restyling glyphs.
world-xiaolai.woff2 extends only the outer-world UI subset with the new map
labels. The original project/resume font assets are not replaced.

cecilia-map.svg is locally authored navigation artwork; cecilia-map.png is
its raster export for the native 3D kiosk. Map destination icons are local
flat illustrations of the portfolio's book, display, lab and postal props.

of the existing portfolio pages supplied for Cecilia Wang's website.
